Fig. X. Mechanistic analysis of a selected beneficial case from the real 1,000-round Semantic-A07 V2 run (round 989, Full receiver 32). (a) Signed class-wise task-response margins against past other-tier references for the top six of 44 eligible classes, ranked by summed evidence u; missing Low-source observations are distinct from zero. The symmetric-log color scale retains all extremes, with a linear interval of +/-0.001 probability. (b) Current gain d, Semantic-Memory-limited evidence m, and Reliability-calibrated U, computed over all 44 classes; calibration is performed per class before averaging. Here n denotes independent current clients per class, while the supporting history is past-only. (c) Absolute A07/V2 contributions and zero-centered share changes in representative shared region R0, shown on their respective percentage and percentage-point scales. (d) Linear-scale paired CE improvements for the selected receiver under all-class and shared-class evaluation; positive values indicate lower CE than same-input A07. The separate context axis shows medians, interquartile ranges, and minimum-to-maximum ranges over all 318 fixed receiver-round cases for every method (symlog, linear within +/-1e-6 CE); diamonds locate the selected all-class case. Asterisks denote numerical-term controls: removing the historical cap or rho factor while retaining eligibility masks. Utility evaluates the full modified region union R0-R6, not R0 alone. This outcome-selected, training-side example illustrates mechanism operation and does not estimate the average 1,000-round benefit.
